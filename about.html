ABOUT US

What is CamConnect?

Finding student competitions in Cambodia used to mean scrolling through five different platforms, joining a dozen Facebook groups, and hoping you didn't miss the deadline by a day.

CamConnect was built to fix that. We aggregate events from across the region, tag them by category, and surface deadlines clearly so you can spend less time searching and more time building.

Whether you're a first-year student looking for your first hackathon or a senior building your portfolio, HackFinder helps you find what's relevant fast.

Every listing is checked before it goes up real deadlines, real organizers, real links. No expired competitions, no dead pages, no guessing whether something is still open.

CamConnect is a class project, built by a student, for students a small step toward making opportunity a little easier to find in Cambodia.

HOW IT WORKS

Find an event in three steps

01
Browse Events

Head to the Competitions page and use the search bar or filter by Level and Category to narrow down events that fit your situation.

02
Check the Detail

Each card shows the event name, category badge, deadline, and eligibility level everything you need to decide quickly.

03
Register Directly

Click the registration link on any card to go straight to the official event page. No middleman, no signups required.
